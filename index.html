Shadow Duel support

Shadow Duel is a hand-shadow puppet game. Every day there's a new shadow to make with your hands; match its shape and your shadow comes to life as a creature. It works entirely on your device — no account, no ads, no tracking.

Contact

Questions, feedback or a problem with the game? E-mail infotech@hexavo.co.uk and include your phone model and the game's version (shown in Me, under About).

Your data

Your progress stays on your phone and the camera video never leaves it. Deleting the game removes everything it saved. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How to play

  • Tap Cast your shadow, prop your phone up so the front camera sees you, and hold your hand (or hands) up about an arm's length away.
  • Move your shadow into the dotted outline on the wall and match its shape. The 10-second clock runs while a hand is in view.
  • Any score brings your creature to life. Tap it to see it react.
  • Play the day's shadow to keep your streak going; practise any shadow you've found in the Zoo.

Tips

  • Play in good, even light, facing the camera, with a plain background behind your hands if you can.
  • If the shadow doesn't appear, check that Shadow Duel is allowed to use the camera in your phone's Settings.
  • The coaching line under the wall tells you which way to move: left, right, up, down, closer or further away.
  • Daily reminders can be turned on or off in Me.
